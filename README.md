## What is Business Intelligence?

Business Intelligence (BI) is about turning raw business data into decisions. A typical BI workflow:

1. **Collect and prepare data**, for example sales transactions or customer records.
2. **Analyse it** to find patterns, such as which products are bought together or which customers are likely to leave.
3. **Present the results** with tables, charts and dashboards.
4. **Recommend an action** and a KPI to measure whether it works.

A core principle is to separate what the data shows (an association) from why it happens (a cause).
